📌 About Our Project

This project analyzes customer reviews from an uploaded dataset and classifies feedback as POSITIVE, NEGATIVE, or NEUTRAL. It helps identify customer satisfaction, complaints, and opinions about products and services.

⚙️ Process: Upload dataset → Analyze reviews → View results.

📂 Supported files: CSV, Excel and text-based PDF.
