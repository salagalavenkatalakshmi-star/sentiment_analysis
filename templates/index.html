📌 About Our Project

Customer Feedback Analysis is a sentiment analysis project that examines customer reviews from an uploaded dataset. It classifies feedback into POSITIVE, NEGATIVE, and NEUTRAL categories. The results help users understand customer opinions, identify satisfaction and complaints, and make better decisions about products and services.

⚙️ How it works: Upload a dataset → Read customer reviews → Analyze sentiment → View results and feedback.

📂 Supported files: CSV, Excel and text-based PDF.
