📌 About Our Project

Customer Feedback Analysis examines reviews from an uploaded dataset and classifies them into POSITIVE, NEGATIVE, and NEUTRAL categories. This helps users understand customer satisfaction, identify complaints, and learn what customers think about products or services.

⚙️ How it works: Upload dataset → Analyze reviews → View sentiment counts and percentages.

📂 Supported formats: CSV, Excel and text-based PDF.

💬 Types of Customer Feedback

😊

POSITIVE

Happy customers and good experiences.

😟

NEGATIVE

Complaints and unsatisfactory experiences.

😐

NEUTRAL

Mixed, unclear or neither-positive-nor-negative feedback.
